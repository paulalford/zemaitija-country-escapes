# Project Brief — Žemaitija Country Escapes

**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-10-23  
**Status:** Approved scope translated into a project-specific repository brief; open decisions tracked in `DECISIONS.md`.

## 1. Purpose and portfolio positioning

Build a **credible three-language transactional booking platform** for a fictional independent rural accommodation operator in the Žemaitija region of Lithuania. The portfolio evidence must come from demonstrable software engineering, rigorous business logic, accessibility, operational design and reproducible tests — not fictional reviews, revenue figures, guests or conversion gains.

The result is not another brochure website. It must allow a test guest to discover a stay, inspect available nights, obtain a quote, complete a test-mode payment, receive booking confirmation, settle the balance, view reminders, manage the reservation and request a governed cancellation/refund.

## 2. Fictional operator and accommodation

Working brand: **Žemaitija Country Escapes**. Working location: countryside in the **Plateliai area, Plungė District**, Lithuania. The town and surrounding tourism sites are real; the operator/property is fictional. Do not invent an occupied address or pin a real property as ours. Public maps must initially indicate an approximate locality only.

Three distinct lodging units, with centralised configurable business attributes:

| Unit | Audience | Maximum guest capacity |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Forest Cottage | Couples | 2 |
| Lakeside Cabin | Couples and nature travellers | 2 |
| Family Cottage | Families or small groups | 6 |

Names and capacities are **fictional design assumptions**. They are not actual property claims. Facilities, extras, photo sets, seasonal rate schedules, accessibility attributes, pets and minimum stays require a single authoritative configuration.

## 3. Markets, languages and content

Lithuanian (`lt`) is the default; English (`en`) and German (`de`) are fully supported. Provide equivalent navigation, accommodation descriptions, guest messages, forms, transactional terms, privacy content and error states. Use appropriately translated URL slugs, stable internal route IDs, localized canonical URLs, `hreflang`, `x-default`, Open Graph metadata and an XML sitemap. Contracts must mean the same thing in all three languages.

Real destination content may cover Lake Plateliai, Žemaitija National Park and established regional cultural/nature sites. Verify factual details against official sources; avoid invented travel time, distance, operating hours, partnerships or endorsements.

## 4. Public product experience

Site modules: Home; accommodation listing and comparison; three accommodation pages; experiences; explore Žemaitija; gallery; practical information; booking/checkout; secure manage-booking page; about; contact; policy/privacy pages; localized error and 404 pages.

Accommodation detail: photos, capacities, sleeping arrangements, amenities, safety/accessibility information, price explanations, stay rules, calendar, extras and prominent booking CTA. Mobile date selection, usable information hierarchy and booking review receive priority.

## 5. Authoritative pricing and availability

Prices are generated by tested server-side rules (unit, dates, weekday/weekend, season, extras, any documented fees/taxes). Calculate nights as calendar-date intervals; departure is exclusive. Store money in **integer EUR cents**. Snapshot the agreed quote and accepted terms for each booking; do not retroactively change them when a seasonal tariff changes.

Inventory comes from PostgreSQL records and is protected by database constraints/transactions. Active compatible checkout holds, paid reservations and operator blocks prevent overlapping bookings. Cancellation or expiry releases inventory only after state reconciliation. No front-end-only availability decisions. Payment and inventory transitions must remain idempotent and auditable.

**Checkout window:** Propose **30 minutes**, aligned to Stripe Checkout's documented 30-minute minimum configured session expiry. Validate behaviour and disable/handle asynchronous payment methods explicitly before implementation. A payment event arriving late must never override an already confirmed booking. See `DECISIONS.md`.

## 6. Financial and booking policy — contractual design, not statutory percentages

- More than 14 days before arrival: **30% advance** to confirm; **70%** due at **T–14**.
- Booking within 14 days of arrival: **100%** payable to confirm.
- Initial payment is an ordinary **advance (`avansas`)**, not automatically legally classified as earnest money (`rankpinigiai`).
- Confirmation requires verified payment status, not a `/success` page redirect.
- At **T–21**, send a warning that remaining payment is due in seven days.
- At **T–14**, send a payment-due notice.
- After deadline, notify overdue guest and allow a **24-hour grace period**. If still unpaid after reconciliation, cancel, release inventory and notify guest. Exact dates and cutoffs are computed in **Europe/Vilnius**, derived from configured local check-in time.
- Guest cancellation **strictly before T–14:** free; refund amounts paid.
- Guest cancellation **at/after T–14:** a **reasonable, supportable charge up to 30%** of total booking value; reduce if saved costs, rebooking or actual loss justify a smaller sum. Ordinary advance is **not automatically forfeited**. The final amount may require operator review and subsequent refund adjustment.
- **No automatic 100% no-show penalty.** No-show handling requires legal and operational review.
- Provider-attributable cancellation: refund all amounts paid; additional compensation subject to legal review and a separately approved contract rule.
- Refund completion follows provider confirmation, with traceable revisions, provider IDs and payment-event reconciliation.

The 30%, 14-day and 24-hour provisions above are **selected business-design terms**, not statutory percentages or durations. A qualified Lithuanian lawyer must approve terms before any live commercial sale.

## 7. Guest experience and notifications

Allow guest to choose unit/dates/guest counts/extras, view itemized price and pay in secure Stripe **test mode**. Before payment prominently show amount due, remaining balance, exact deadline, cancellation cutoff, cancellation charge policy and terms acceptance. Checkout submission must unambiguously convey payment obligation in each language.

A secure emailed manage-booking link (short-lived, unpredictable and scoped) allows the guest to inspect payment status, pay the balance, request cancellation and see refund status. Date changes can be deferred beyond initial scope.

Durable notification outbox/attempt log: advance confirmation, balance reminder, due and overdue notices, paid-in-full confirmation, guest/provider/nonpayment cancellation, refund confirmation and arrival information. Deduplicate retries and track failures. Scheduled tasks must tolerate late, repeated and interrupted runs; webhook reconciliation precedes any cancellation for nonpayment.

## 8. Administration and operations

Authenticated and authorized admin area for occupancy calendar, availability blocks, booking search, amount due/overdue queues, cancellation assessment, refunds, pricing/extras changes, notification re-send, event audit and operational health. Audit each inventory/financial change. Do not build a public demo with unrestricted admin privileges.

## 9. Proposed stack

- Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- PostgreSQL, Drizzle ORM and explicit SQL for critical inventory constraints
- Stripe Checkout in test mode, server-side validated event processing
- A reliable scheduled job/worker and durable notification outbox
- A transaction email provider in sandbox/test configuration
- Zod, Vitest and Playwright
- Leaflet/OpenStreetMap for approximate location and actual nearby attractions
- Vercel-compatible application deployment; provider choice reviewed for scheduled work

Use the minimum necessary services. Do not store raw card numbers. No live payment keys or guest personal data in source control.

### Mandatory language and runtime restriction

**PHP is prohibited across the entire application and development pipeline.** No PHP code, PHP-based CMS, PHP payment/booking plugins, PHP server endpoints, PHP scheduled workers, or PHP dependencies are permitted. Build public UI, admin UI, API handlers, payment webhooks, and background application logic using the chosen **TypeScript/Next.js** architecture with PostgreSQL. External payment/email providers are allowed; no PHP runtime is required or permitted within the project.

## 10. Legal and privacy guardrails

Working guidance: Lithuanian consumer-contract law distinguishes dated accommodation from the ordinary distance-contract withdrawal window, scrutinizes disproportionately onerous consumer terms, requires clarity around online payment obligations, and distinguishes advance payments from legally defined earnest money. Lithuanian accommodation operations and tourist reporting require their own compliance checks, including e.turistas where applicable. Do not treat the fictional operation as licensed, registered or authorised.

Before real commerce, require Lithuanian legal review of the contractual terms, price/tax displays, cancellation assessment, refund timing, provider-cancellation remedies, privacy, data retention, guest registration, regulatory classification, and dispute information.

Portfolio mode uses only synthetic lodging, guests and Stripe test payments. Provide obvious fictional-business disclosure. No invented client testimony, star ratings, conversion rates, occupancy, reviews, certificates or real address.

## 11. Hard engineering acceptance criteria

1. Concurrent overlapping paid bookings are prevented at the database level.
2. Hold, payment, confirmation, cancellation and refund events are correctly reconciled after retries, timeouts and out-of-order delivery.
3. T–21 and T–14 reminders, grace period and precise cutoff boundaries pass deterministic clock tests across Lithuanian DST transitions.
4. Refund arithmetic and conditional late charges are auditable and do not silently retain the full advance.
5. The public UI, checkout and customer notices work in LT/EN/DE; accessibility and mobile booking flow are tested.
6. No consumer is asked to pay real money in portfolio mode.
7. A clean environment reproduces application/database/test fixtures and meaningful integration/E2E tests.
8. Operational, legal and portfolio disclosures are consistent with actual functionality.
