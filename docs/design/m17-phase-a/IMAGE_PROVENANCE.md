## Production-image gate

Prefer genuine destination photographs when a future approved design names an attraction. For each selected file, retain original source and file URL, photographer, rights holder, license version or written permission, allowed commercial/derivative use, attribution, any conditions and retrieval date. Public visibility, government hosting, a search thumbnail and a credit line alone do not establish reusable rights. Do not hotlink unverified photographs or crop institutional logos as endorsements.

If production uses generated atmosphere, caption it as **“AI-generated regional illustration; not a depiction of this attraction.”** Keep the adjacent caption on an opaque background, available to assistive technology as ordinary text. Never pair an invented building with wording implying that it documents Beržoras, a specific museum or palace. A genuine photo and its approved caption/alt text are separate review decisions after concept approval.
