# Destination content inventory

Research date: **27 October 2026**. Phase A editorial research only; no application content or translations changed. The official pages linked in each row are the evidence for that row, rather than a general bibliography. English copy below is proposed, not approved. Lithuanian names retain diacritics. No visitor hours, prices, transport timetables, accessibility guarantees or accommodation-origin distances are proposed.

The three groups are editorial scopes, not travel-time bands or ethnographic boundaries. “Around Plateliai” includes the wider park locality, including Žemaičių Kalvarija; it does not mean every place is walkable. “Major excursions” describes a separate planning decision, without implying a measured journey length.

## Regional and editorial controls

[The Ethnic Culture Council's map](https://www.ekgt.lt/etnografiniai-regionai/zemelapis/) distinguishes five ethnographic regions and explains that cultural boundaries differ from administrative ones and have transitional areas. Its current page records a 13 May 2025 revision. A tourism brand's geographic coverage is not evidence of ethnographic membership. Do not draw a hard “Žemaitija” border around every excursion. Hill of Crosses is labelled by its verified district; a precise local ethnographic classification remains a research decision, not an inferred fact. Klaipėda/Curonian Spit extensions would require separate Lithuania Minor/coastal qualification and are not proposed as core Samogitian heritage here.

**Creative interpretation:** hierarchy, section titles, lighting, illustrations, crops, colours, map styling, selection and ordering. They do not establish actual scenery, architecture, a recommended itinerary or access rights. The green/paper design is contemporary Timber Index, not a claim about traditional regional graphic design.

**Source caution:** official pages contain stale schedules, inconsistent translated place names and conflicting coordinates. This inventory deliberately retains the conflicts instead of resolving them by guesswork. The inventory is independently reviewable at claim level; unresolved pins are excluded from production readiness. Recheck mutable visitor information with each destination before a future publication. Link out with “Official visitor information”; the fictional operator neither sells nor manages these experiences.
